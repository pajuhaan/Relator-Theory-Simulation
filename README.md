## What is Relator Theory

Relator Theory is a two-space framework where a complex generator space \(\mathbb{C}\) and a real projection space \(\mathbb{R}^3\) are linked by a fundamental phase constraint (the “lock”)

\(R\omega = c\).

In this view, familiar physical quantities and bounds are treated as *emergent* from geometry + phase consistency rather than assumed as independent axioms. The goal of this repo is practical: provide **interactive, intuitive experiments** that complement the papers and make the dynamics easier to reason about.
